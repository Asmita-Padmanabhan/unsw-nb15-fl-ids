LIMITATIONS THAT MUST APPEAR IN THE PAPER
- Preprocessing is shared (fitted on pooled training sample); only model training is federated.
- Client heterogeneity is simulated with a Dirichlet label-skew over traffic families, not measured from real tenants.
- UNSW-NB15 raw data come from two capture days; the temporal split therefore mixes a day-level shift with within-day drift.
- Hyper-parameters were fixed a priori, not tuned; the validation partition is reserved and untouched (no model selection).
- Random stratified split is a baseline, not an IID guarantee: related flows/campaigns can fall on both sides.
- Training-paradigm claims are restricted to the budget-matched methods (centralized_matched, local, fedavg); centralized_es is a reference only.
- Temporal protocol = chronological holdout with a seeded, label-stratified subsample of the historical training block.
- Dirichlet partitions are single pure draws (no size rejection): empty/tiny clients occur at small alpha and are reported; local_macro and local_weighted answer different questions (per-node vs traffic-level).
- Per-family results are detection recall of a binary detector; per-family precision/F1 are not defined and are not reported.
- Exact duplicates can make evaluation optimistic when identical records cross the evaluation boundary; they are not asserted to be leakage per se.
- FedAvg clients restart Adam every round whereas local/centralized models keep optimizer state across epochs.
- Not a privacy-preserving FL pipeline: preprocessing statistics are computed centrally.
- With 5 seeds, paired tests have limited power (exact Wilcoxon cannot go below p = 0.0625); interpret effect sizes and intervals, not p-values alone.
- The unseen-family experiment is AUXILIARY: it measures how a binary detector handles a held-out attack family, NOT zero-day detection.
- One dataset, one architecture (MLP, chosen deliberately to isolate the training paradigm) and one aggregation rule (FedAvg): conclusions hold for the evaluated MLP only. No novelty claim is made until the literature check is done.
